Products and pricing

Current full-size TABAQ kits are priced at R1,750 for South African checkout and USD 107 for international checkout. International delivery is quoted separately. Where an international delivery quote is converted into USD, the courier component may use the current Wise USD/ZAR reference when the secure payment link is created. The kit price itself remains USD 107. The final amount payable is the amount displayed on the secure payment page.

Orders

An order is not complete until payment has been successfully confirmed. We may decline or cancel an order if stock is unavailable, payment cannot be confirmed, delivery is not possible to the destination, or there is an obvious pricing or technical error. If payment has already been received for an order we cannot fulfil, the affected amount will be refunded.

Delivery

South African orders containing two or more full-size kits qualify for free standard delivery. One-kit South African orders and all international orders require an exact delivery quote before payment unless a specific delivery amount is shown in checkout. See the Delivery page.

International orders

A shipping quote does not guarantee that every destination can accept every TABAQ product. Shipment availability is confirmed before payment. Unless expressly included in your quote, destination duties, taxes, brokerage and import charges are not included.
