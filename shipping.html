Customer care

Delivery without guesswork.

We do not hide variable delivery costs inside the product price. The exact delivery charge is confirmed before payment whenever it is not free.

South Africa

2 or more full-size kits: standard delivery is free to eligible South African delivery addresses.

1 full-size kit: delivery is charged separately. Submit your destination from the cart and we will confirm the exact delivery charge before payment.

International

International delivery is quoted per order because the cost varies by destination and shipment. Submit your country, city and postal code from the cart. We confirm the exact delivery amount before any payment is taken.

Current full-size kits are priced at R1,750 for South Africa and USD 107 for international checkout. International delivery is quoted separately because courier cost varies by destination. If a USD payment link is issued, only the quoted delivery charge may be converted using the current Wise USD/ZAR reference at the time the link is created; the kit price remains USD 107.

Some destinations may not be available for the full-size kit. We confirm shipment availability before payment.

How the quote process works

  1. Add the kit or kits you want to your cart.
  2. Select South Africa or International delivery.
  3. If a quote is required, submit your destination details.
  4. We confirm the exact delivery charge. For a USD payment link, the kit remains USD 107 and the quoted courier cost is converted at the Wise USD/ZAR reference captured when the link is created.
  5. We send a secure payment link for the product total plus the agreed delivery amount.
  6. No delivery charge is taken before you see the amount.

International duties and import charges

Unless your delivery quote expressly says otherwise, destination-country duties, taxes, brokerage fees or other import charges are not included in the TABAQ product price or delivery quote. These charges, where applicable, are determined outside TABAQ's checkout.
