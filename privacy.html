Privacy

How TABAQ handles your information.

We collect only the information needed to operate the store, process orders, respond to requests and manage optional launch updates.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit, such as name, email and phone number.
  • Delivery information used for shipping quotes and order fulfilment.
  • Cart and order details.
  • Messages you send through the contact form.
  • Email addresses submitted to the optional drop list.

Payments

Payment-card details are handled by our payment provider during secure checkout. TABAQ does not need to store your full card number in this website's order records.

Why we use your information

We use submitted information to provide quotes, process and fulfil orders, provide customer support, manage returns, prevent misuse and send launch updates where you have asked to receive them.

Storage and retention

Order and customer-service records are kept only for as long as reasonably required for fulfilment, support, accounting, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Cart preferences may be stored locally in your browser so your basket can persist between pages.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information where applicable, or ask to stop receiving optional marketing messages. Use the contact form and choose Privacy request.

Service providers and cross-border processing

We may use service providers for payment processing, hosting, messaging, fulfilment and delivery. Information is shared only where reasonably necessary to provide those services or meet legal obligations.
